The journey

Francesco Scarselli was born in Campobasso in 2006 and began playing the diatonic accordion at six. In 2020 he discovered jazz accordion and his current teacher, Maestro Renzo Ruggieri.

In 2021 he won the World Music category in Klingenthal; in 2023, within two months, the three leading international accordion competitions: the Coupe Mondiale CIA, the Premio Internazionale della Fisarmonica and the Trofeo Mondiale CMA.

He now studies at the G. Braga Conservatory in Teramo and performs on his Bugari accordion, model 815/FR.
