The journey

Francesco Scarselli was born in Campobasso in 2006. He began playing the diatonic accordion at six, turning an early passion into a rigorous musical education.

In 2020 he discovered jazz accordion and his teacher, Maestro Renzo Ruggieri. He now studies at the G. Braga Conservatory in Teramo and performs on his Bugari accordion, model 815/FR.

In 2021 he took first place in the World Music category, open to all ages, at the Internationaler Akkordeonwettbewerb in Klingenthal, Germany, performing on the diatonic accordion.

In 2023, within two months, he won the three leading international accordion competitions — the Coupe Mondiale CIA, the Premio Internazionale della Fisarmonica and the Trofeo Mondiale CMA — in their respective Junior categories.
