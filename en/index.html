Who he is

Francesco Scarselli was born in Campobasso in 2006 and grew up in Campochiaro. He started playing the diatonic accordion at six and was a world champion at eleven.

In 2020 he found jazz accordion, and his teacher, Renzo Ruggieri. He now studies at the Gaetano Braga Conservatory in Teramo and plays a Bugari Armando 815/FR.

Over two months in 2023 he won the three most important competitions in the world, one after the other.
