Biography

Francesco Scarselli was born in Campobasso on 15 April 2006 and grew up in Campochiaro. He began playing the diatonic accordion at six, setting out on an artistic path enriched over time by study, concert performance and international awards.

Between 2015 and 2019 he won Category A at the Festival dell'Adriatico four years in a row. He also won the World Championship in Montescaglioso in 2015, with 99.50 out of 100, and the Bologna edition in 2016, with 99 out of 100, in his category. In 2016 he received the Bimbo Regione Molise award from the President of the Molise Regional Council. In 2017 he appeared on Canale 5's The Little Big Show with Gerry Scotti; in 2018 he joined the Sicilian Tour of the World Accordion Festival; in 2019 he won the Accordeon Art Festival in Roseto degli Abruzzi.

In 2020 M° Igor Zavadsky invited him to Kyiv for two concerts. That same year he began studying jazz and variété accordion with M° Renzo Ruggieri. In 2021 he won the World Music category, open to all ages, at the Internationaler Akkordeonwettbewerb in Klingenthal, on the diatonic accordion.

That same year brought first place in 15 & Under Variety at the British College of Accordionists and, at the 12th Italia Award, the Gold Medal in Variété together with the Art Accordion category. In 2022 came victory at Accordion Star International, the Laszlo Spezzaferri International Music Prize with 100 out of 100, another 100 out of 100 at the Concorso Musicale Internazionale Paolo Barrasso in Caramanico Terme, and first place in the Junior category, again at the British College of Accordionists.

In 2023, within two months, he won three of the world's leading international accordion competitions: the Coupe Mondiale CIA in Bijeljina on 9 September, with 24.07 out of 25; the Premio Internazionale della Fisarmonica in Castelfidardo on 14 September, with 19.85 out of 20; and the Trofeo Mondiale CMA on 4 November.

He now studies at the G. Braga Conservatory in Teramo, plays a Bugari 815/FR accordion and brings jazz and variété accordion to concert stages in Italy and abroad.
