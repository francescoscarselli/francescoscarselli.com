Privacy and statistics

This site stores no personal information, registrations or user profiles.

Data and consent

Visit statistics are handled by Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) acting as data processor. The script is not loaded before you choose: the legal basis for the processing is consent.

If you accept, Google Analytics collects in aggregate form the pages visited, the length of the visit, the country of origin, the referral source, the device and the browser. The IP address is anonymised. Google retains the data for the period set on the property, up to fourteen months, and it may be transferred to the United States under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Managing preferences

Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time from this page or from the link at the bottom of every page. Clearing this site's data in your browser resets the preference.

Services not used

There is no advertising, no social button with tracking functions and no typeface loaded from an external server.

Rights and contact
